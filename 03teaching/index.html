Teaching

Over the past twenty years, I've worked in elementary and secondary schools, a language school, colleges, universities, and a variety of community-based settings. Here are some examples:

ABE Fundamentals

Instructor
Vancouver Community College
2013-2025

Taught fundamental-level English, math, and computer studies. Supported hundreds of adult learners in building foundational skills, confidence, and readiness for further education and employment.

Rabble Rousers

Founder & Facilitator
Coast Mental Health
2018-2020

Founded and facilitated the Coast Mental Health Rabble Rousers program, empowering young adults in recovery to become advocates for change in the mental health system.

Queer, Trans & Two-Spirit Upgrading Class

Instructor
Vancouver Community College
2016-2018

Created the first adult upgrading class of its kind in Canada. A culturally safe pathway for learners to complete upgrading in an affirming setting.

New Westminster School District 40

Teacher on Call
2013-2014

Provided K-12 instruction across diverse learning environments, including a term contract at the innovative Island Discovery Learning Community on Bowen Island.

Community Education Programs

Facilitator & Tutor
Simon Fraser University
2011-2013

Facilitated and tutored in a variety of programs including the Aboriginal Bridge Program, Aboriginal Pre-Health Program, and the Community Capacity Building Certificate.

International Language Academy of Canada

Instructor
2008-2009

Taught English language classes for international learners. Promoted to Head EAL Teacher based on leadership and instructional practice.

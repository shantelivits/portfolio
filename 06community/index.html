Community Organizing

I've worked alongside others to expand access to health care, strengthen food security, support mental health, advance systems change, and promote human rights.

Catherine White Holman Wellness Centre

Organizer · 2011-2021 · Vancouver, BC

Played a lead role in establishing Canada's first wellness clinic run entirely by and for trans, Two-Spirit, and gender-diverse people. Helped establish the informed-consent model of care now recognized as best practice across BC.

Trans Community Kitchen

Co-founder · 2014-2018 · Vancouver, BC

Forged a partnership with Saige Community Food Bank providing community meals, skill-building, and a place for trans and nonbinary people to build connection in a safe, welcoming environment.

Wellness Retreat for Trans Sex Workers

Lead Organizer · 2017 · Vancouver, BC

Secured a grant and developed a trauma-informed program with PACE Society that offered holistic wellness supports and community care for trans sex workers in a safe, affirming space.

Trans Day of Advocacy

Lead Organizer · 2014 · Province-wide

Played a lead role in organizing a grassroots advocacy initiative that directly contributed to the establishment of BC's first province-wide transgender health program: Trans Care BC.

BC Safer Schools Coalition

Organizer · 2014 · Vancouver, BC

Part of a community coalition that secured a SOGI-inclusive policy at the Vancouver School Board, helping set a precedent for districts across B.C.

Provincial Election Campaign

Campaign Office Organizer · 2011 · Vancouver, BC

Coordinated daily operations for a busy campaign office, managing information flow, volunteer direction, and front-of-house activity. Our candidate eventually became Premier of British Columbia.
